About

I'm a product designer who likes problems with a lot of moving parts. The kind that need a system, not just a screen. Most of my work starts in Figma and ends up somewhere between a UI kit and a small piece of information architecture.

Outside of client and school work, I spend a good amount of time on typography: building specimen sheets, testing letterforms, and generally treating type as a design problem in its own right rather than a settings menu.

Currently studying at the University of Lagos, splitting time between coursework and design projects like the ones below.

How I work

  • 01

    Understand the system first

    Before any screen, I map what already exists (the data, the roles, the edge cases) so the design fits the real shape of the problem.

  • 02

    Sketch wide, then narrow

    I explore a few genuinely different directions early, so the final structure is a choice, not the only option I thought of.

  • 03

    Build it as a system

    Components, states, and spacing get defined once and reused, so the file stays consistent as it grows past the first few screens.

  • 04

    Hand off with detail

    Specs, states, and edge cases are documented clearly enough that engineering doesn't have to guess.
