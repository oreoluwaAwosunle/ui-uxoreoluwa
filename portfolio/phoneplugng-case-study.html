PhonePlugNG: Catalog to Chat

A 120-item phone resale catalog built to do one job well: get someone from browsing to a WhatsApp chat about the exact item they want, fast.

TypeFounder & product owner
FocusE-commerce, catalog UX
ToolsHTML, CSS, JavaScript
01

The business, and the gap

PhonePlugNG (formerly Olympus Gadgets) sells IMEI-verified phones, laptops, and accessories out of Lagos, entirely through WhatsApp and Instagram. That's where the real business happens, but it meant every inventory question, "do you have this," "how much," "is it still available," started from zero in a DM, with no way for someone to browse first.

I built this landing page to front-load that browsing, so a WhatsApp conversation starts with someone who's already found the specific item they want, not someone fishing in the dark.

120
items across 9 categories
1
tap from any listing to a pre-filled WhatsApp message
0
backend, forms, or accounts required to browse or ask
02

A catalog you can actually search

All 120 items are listed exactly as they'd be quoted in person, structured from a raw price-list spreadsheet into a searchable, filterable list. Real-time search, nine category filters, and sort by price or name, all client-side, no backend. Typing "MacBook" narrows 120 items down to the 16 that match instantly.

03

Every item is one tap from a real conversation

Each listing's "Ask →" button opens WhatsApp with a message already filled in, referencing that exact item and price. No contact form, no email, no generic "get in touch." The design decision was to meet customers on the channel the business already runs on, and to make sure the conversation starts with context instead of "hi, do you have iPhones."

This mirrors how the business actually operates day to day: a founder-run shop where trust and speed of response matter more than a polished checkout flow. The design follows that reality rather than fighting it.

04

Trust, stated up front

Buying used or gray-market electronics in Nigeria comes with real, well-founded anxiety: is the IMEI clean, what happens if it breaks in a week, will the price change once you show up. The page addresses all three directly rather than leaving them for a DM to surface.

05

Ownership, stated plainly

This one's mine end to end, not a client project. I run PhonePlugNG, designed and built this page myself, structured the catalog from a raw spreadsheet, and wrote the WhatsApp-first outreach approach that the whole page is built around. If it's wrong, that's on me; if it works, that's mine too.
