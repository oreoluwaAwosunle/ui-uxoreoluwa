Case study · AI concept

Fit Check

An AI research assistant that checks a job posting against my real CV, explains the gaps instead of scoring keywords, and flags postings that contradict themselves.

TypePersonal exploration, active job search
FocusEvidence synthesis, confidence, contradiction detection
ToolsHTML / JS prototyping

FIG 1 · A stretch verdict, with each requirement read and explained on its own line

01

Where it came from

A posting can say “junior friendly, fast paced” in one line and “5+ years required” three lines down. Keyword matching treats every line as equally true. Reading a posting properly means deciding which lines to actually trust, and that's a comprehension task, not a search task.

02

The problem

I was checking postings by scanning for terms like “product designer,” “UI/UX,” and pay. That tells me the words are present, not whether I actually fit, and it says nothing about a posting that's quietly inconsistent with itself.

03

04

How it solves it

A keyword scan can't tell “3+ years required” from “proven experience designing interfaces”
→
reads the actual requirement, not just the words in it
A flat yes or no hides how close a real fit actually is
→
per-requirement breakdown, matched or gap, each one explained
A gap gets read as “don't apply”
→
narrates what a CV at that requirement level typically includes, as reference, never edits my real CV
Postings that contradict themselves get read at face value
→
flags the inconsistency directly, junior title with senior requirements, so I apply with the real picture instead of the posted one
